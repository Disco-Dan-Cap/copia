# iPhone capture guide — stills + walkthrough video

Click-by-click for the six proof stills (per DAY_14_DEVICE_CHECKLIST.md) and the app walkthrough video. **Order matters** — the install invitation only shows BEFORE you install, so follow the sequence.

## Prep (2 minutes)

1. **Silence interruptions:** swipe down from the top-right corner → tap the Focus (moon) button → Do Not Disturb. A banner notification mid-recording ruins a take.
2. **Enable screen recording** (if you've never used it): Settings → Control Center → scroll to "Screen Recording" → tap the green +.
3. **Clean home screen:** swipe to a home-screen page that isn't cluttered — the installed Copia icon shot looks best among a handful of apps, not 30.
4. Open **Safari** → go to **copia-virid.vercel.app**. Let it fully load.
5. Reminders: screenshot = **Side button + Volume Up** pressed together. Stop a recording = tap the **red status bar/clock** → Stop.

## Part 1 — The install invitation (BEFORE installing — it never shows after)

The invitation appears on your **second visit**. To trigger it: browse the site briefly, close the tab (or quit Safari), then open a new tab and visit the site again.

1. On the second visit, wait a moment — the invitation card appears ("Keep Copia on your home screen").
2. **📸 STILL 1:** screenshot the invitation as it renders in Safari.
3. Do NOT tap "Not now" — leave it for the next step.

*If the invitation won't appear (already dismissed in the past), open:* `copia-virid.vercel.app/?__pwaPreview=invite` *— the test parameter forces it.*

## Part 2 — The Share-sheet step

1. Tap Safari's **Share button** (the square with the up-arrow, bottom center).
2. Scroll the sheet up slightly until **"Add to Home Screen"** is visible.
3. **📸 STILL 2:** screenshot the share sheet with "Add to Home Screen" in frame.

## Part 3 — Install + icon

1. Tap **Add to Home Screen** → the name "Copia" should be pre-filled → tap **Add**.
2. Find the Copia leaf icon on your home screen.
3. **📸 STILL 3:** screenshot the home screen — the leaf tile among your other apps.

## Part 4 — The launch + splash (use video, grab the frame)

The splash lasts about a second — too fast for a clean screenshot. Record it instead:

1. Swipe down from top-right → tap the **record button** (circle-in-circle) → you get a 3-second countdown.
2. Go to the home screen → tap the **Copia icon** → let it launch fully (static splash → wordmark assembles → app appears).
3. Tap the red clock → **Stop**. The video saves to Photos.
4. In Photos, open the video, pause on the splash frame → **📸 STILL 4** via screenshot (or use the video itself in the portfolio — it's the better artifact).
5. With the app open: **📸 STILL 5:** the app running full-screen — no Safari bars, no browser chrome.

## Part 5 — The walkthrough video (the portfolio centerpiece)

One continuous recording, ~60–90 seconds. Practice the path once before recording.

1. Start a screen recording (Control Center → record → countdown).
2. Launch Copia from the icon (captures the splash again).
3. Browse Home briefly → open a listing → **Add to basket** (watch the button settle).
4. Add two more items **from two different growers** (the seeded multi-grower path).
5. Open the **basket** — linger a beat on "THREE THINGS FROM THREE GROWERS."
6. Tap **Settle up**. On the checkout page, slowly:
   - Toggle one grower between pickup and delivery — **watch the narration sentence rewrite itself** (this is the money shot; give it 2–3 seconds per state).
   - If two growers share a market: pause on the one-trip suggestion line.
   - Tap **USDC** — pause on the grower-economics line — tap back to Card.
7. Tap **Settle up** → the calm settling state → **"It's arranged."** confirmation. Pause 2 seconds.
8. Go to **Orders** — the new arrangement at the top. Stop recording.

**📸 STILL 6** (if not grabbed already): any frame you love from this flow — the checkout with the narration line is the strongest candidate.

## Part 6 — Get it all to your Mac

1. Photos → select the stills + videos → Share → **AirDrop** to your Mac.
2. Drop everything into `case-study/screenshots/device/` in the EDEN folder.
3. Tell Claude Code (or me) they're in — the hero swap slot in the portfolio page is marked and waiting.

## Don'ts

- Don't film in low battery mode (yellow battery in every shot).
- Don't rush the checkout toggles — the narration rewriting is the whole demo.
- Don't worry about a perfect single take; record the flow twice and keep the better one.
